# Child-friendly gameplay and accessibility report

Implemented on `feat/child-friendly-gameplay-interactions`, starting from commit `38e594f`. Tracked code was clean at inspection; the 24 new PNGs supplied by the user were untracked. They are preserved unchanged. No commit, push, merge or reset was performed. No dependency, Vite configuration, NASA data or science-classification change was made.

1. **Files changed:** the exact file list is in [child-friendly-files.txt](child-friendly-files.txt). A owns story, HUD, feedback, voice, introduction, exploration and result presentation. B owns map cards and gait. C owns the existing reducer/action limit and save-action allowlist. D owns atomic preparation/randomization and existing mission validation copy. E owns tests, artwork documentation and this report. Cross-feature contracts were recorded in [integration-contract.md](integration-contract.md).
2. **Randomized preparation:** a pure injectable random source distributes safe reserves within the remaining manifest capacity, using the existing 0–10 scale and cargo configuration. Minimums are derived from suggested supplies: 5 Energy and 4 of each other supply. Existing equipment stays packed. `SET_RANDOMIZED_SUPPLIES` validates and applies the edit atomically, clears Mission Control review and never confirms cargo. Quantities remain manually editable; BAI offers the requested new-plan message. Saved journals contain quantities rather than random seeds.
3. **Low-resource warnings:** each reserve at 2 or below has an icon, actual count and warning styling. All indicators stay visible in either HUD mode. The warning list prioritizes the lowest quantity, then air/energy/water/food. BAI reads real production readiness, including energy costs, daily locks, unavailable facilities and exhausted Actions. Alert episodes avoid repeating unchanged warnings; recovery clears or updates them. Existing zero-resource terminal rules remain unchanged.
4. **Outdoor motion:** the existing SVG astronaut has a 3 px hop with a longer airborne interval, a gentle landing compression and a grounded shadow. Indoor walking uses the ordinary gait. Speeds, coordinates, collision meshes and pathfinding are unchanged. Lead and companion animation obey system and in-game reduced-motion preferences.
5. **Building interactions:** mouse hover and keyboard focus open persistent quick cards. Tapping/clicking opens the information panel immediately and uses the existing walk route when EVA is allowed. Cards show actual condition, supported production, costs, readiness and scene-entry actions. Greenhouse checks Food, solar checks Energy, and habitat equipment checks Oxygen or Water separately. The lab offers science work, never fake supply production. Escape/outside clicks dismiss information; hover cards sit at the sides clear of other buildings and core controls.
6. **BAI blocked-action guidance:** reusable `ActionButton` uses `aria-disabled` so blocked gameplay actions can still be focused or attempted. It exposes the engine reason in visible text, title and accessible description, and routes attempted activation to BAI's explanation and next-step button. Invalid actions never dispatch through this control; reducers also retain validation. Help closes on panel changes rather than retaining stale advice. Reducer rejections also feed BAI with the attempted action and phase-appropriate recovery, including unaffordable landing choices.
7. **Voice narration:** no recorded voice assets were found. User-triggered Web Speech playback supports the current caption, replay, mute and three speeds. English voices are selected per character when available; BAI is pronounced “Bay.” One controller cancels earlier speech, ignores stale callbacks and stops on advancing lines, closing panels or changing scenes. Text is always visible. Missing or failed speech never blocks gameplay. Voice preferences are local UI settings, separate from the saved mission.
8. **Mars Day terminology:** active screens, guidance, result history and legacy presentation copy use Mars Day. Internal `sol` fields, IDs, actions and save keys remain compatible. The mission introduction explains the longer day.
9. **Mission-log navigation:** each objective is actionable. The existing prerequisite graph identifies the first unfinished requirement; actual engine readiness supplies blockers. Guidance closes the log, opens site/planning controls or requests a collision-aware route toward the site/lab. Destinations are highlighted. Navigation does not carry a mission command, spend an Action or grant completion. Completed objectives open the report view. If EVA is blocked, laboratory work is available through the base console instead of an outdoor crew route.
10. **Main introduction:** new adventures open a large illustrated mission card with three visual goals and Start Adventure. It supports optional BAI speech and Help replay. It is presentation over preparation, not a new gameplay phase. Continue does not reopen it.
11. **Exploration setup:** site choice is step 1; illustrated team selection is step 2; sampling and equipment are step 3; real readiness and Mission Control review are step 4; Begin Exploration is step 5. Each decision appears separately. Optional equipment stays tied to confirmed cargo; standard capabilities remain listed. Confirmation and expedition validation cannot be bypassed.
12. **Three Actions:** the authoritative limit, initialization and daily reset now use 3. Costs remain unchanged. A fourth job is rejected without deductions, and no scene switch refills Actions. Version 4 histories replay under the new capacity while preserving paid work, resource transactions, missions and consumption records; an active old save gets the additional unused third Action. New saves round-trip without gaining Actions on reload.
13. **HUD placement:** Actions sits beside the resource meters on desktop, with separate spacing, actual remaining/maximum counts and three accessible filled/empty symbols. Narrow screens place it in a dedicated row adjacent to the resource row. A ResizeObserver keeps the objective below the actual header height, including warning and accessibility text changes.
14. **HUD guide:** four short BAI steps highlight resources, Actions, the real habitat and Mission log. Next and Skip always remain available. Completion uses the existing saved dialogue-acknowledgment contract and grants no gameplay effect. Help can replay it.
15. **Science review and endings:** the report shows the actual sample, an explicit result, a short interpretation, speech and a next/report action. Technical data is collapsed. Supporting ice evidence, no ice detected, inconclusive and incomplete analysis have distinct truthful copy. Negative/uncertain results are useful science. Endings prioritize the recorded outpost outcome and confirmed zero reserve, then a resource graphic and practical suggestion. Missing samples, analysis or reports explain unfinished ICEBOUND separately from survival success. The crew is represented reassuringly at base.
16. **Artwork:** all 24 documented new slots point to supplied PNGs. [public/assets/README.md](../public/assets/README.md) lists every required filename, inspected dimensions and intended display shape. New screens use the registry and PixelImage; missing primary/fallback art becomes a styled accessible symbol. Neutral result symbols avoid turning the alarm-style no-ice illustration into a gameplay-failure signal. No image was generated or fabricated.
17. **Automated validation:** 270 tests passed, 0 failed. `npm run build`, `npm run lint`, `npm test` and `git diff --check` pass. The suite covers 1,000 randomized distributions, ten randomized real-engine survival/investigation runs, atomic review invalidation, editable/locked cargo, 0–10 and capacity limits, three Actions and rejection, save replay, warning priority/blockers/recovery, objective prerequisites, ending causes, supplied art and speech lifecycle. Existing engine, science, navigation and component tests remain passing.
18. **Browser validation:** actual installed Chrome/Playwright runs cover 1366×768, 1920×1080, 1024×768 and 390×844. Normal gameplay has no page-level scrolling at those sizes. Mouse, keyboard, touch emulation, side-card access, outside/Escape close, correct resource checks, actual frame-traced outdoor/indoor/reduced motion, warning deduplication/recovery, three paid Actions, blocked jobs, guide replay, save/resume, a normal two-site ten-day playthrough and natural depletion failure were exercised. Additional science screens use valid reducer-journal fixtures, not injected resource/outcome state. Missing intro, expedition and science art and unsupported voice were deliberately tested. See [browser-results.json](qa/child-friendly/browser-results.json) and [movement-traces.json](qa/child-friendly/movement-traces.json).
19. **Known limitations:** browser speech voices and pronunciation vary by device; browser lifecycle tests use a deterministic speech adapter and do not certify audible output or screen-reader behavior. Touch uses emulation, not a physical device. Small screens may scroll inside building cards, reports and ending content while keeping primary controls accessible. Existing repairs, power allocation and storm operations remain unimplemented and are never fabricated. Previously supplied high-resolution assets remain unchanged; mobile image optimization is future work. No child usability study or independent owner sign-off is claimed.
20. **PR readiness:** implementation, regression checks, screenshots and documentation are ready for review. Include the supplied untracked artwork and new source/test files when preparing a commit. C/D/A/B/E should review their affected contracts before merge. This task leaves the branch and all changes uncommitted as requested.
